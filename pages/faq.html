Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about portal access, course registrations, student records, and platform security.

StudentHub is the university's centralized student management and academic collaboration ecosystem. All currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students, faculty members, and academic counselors can access the platform with their institutional email credentials.

Head to the Registration Page, input your full name, student university email, valid mobile number, enrolled course, and year of study. Once validated, your credentials will be linked to your student profile immediately.

Visit the Events Page, view the schedule and venue for your preferred workshop or cultural fest, and click the Register action button. Confirmation and schedule reminders will appear directly in your dashboard notices.

StudentHub strictly adheres to ISO-certified institutional data standards. All personal information, submitted grades, and contact details are stored within secure, encrypted servers accessible only by authorized university department registrars.

If you experience password resets or authentication problems, contact the IT helpdesk through the Contact Page or send an email directly to support@studenthub.com.
